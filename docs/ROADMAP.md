## V2: Commercial workflow

- Products, variants, and price lists
- Quotes and PDF generation
- Orders and documents
- Email integration
- Advanced reporting

## V3: Automation and intelligence

- Email synchronization
- Calendar integration
- Automated reminders and inactivity alerts
- Customer history summaries
- Suggested next actions
- Quote assistance and sales insights

The V1 scope is intentionally focused on the salesperson's daily workspace. AI and integrations remain optional advisors and must not block core CRM workflows.
